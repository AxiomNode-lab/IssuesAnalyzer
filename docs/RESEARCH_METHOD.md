# Research Method

## Scope
Competitor and demand scan covering public GitHub repositories, product sites, official GitHub documentation, and relevant Reddit discussions as of 2026-08-25.

## Method
1. Search variants of good-first-issue finder, issue recommender/matcher, opportunity scoring, and repository health.
2. Inspect direct product pages or repositories where available.
3. Separate direct competitors from adjacent tools.
4. Record user anecdotes only as qualitative signals.
5. Avoid claims of uniqueness or demand without interviews and behavioral evidence.

## Limitations
Search ranking is incomplete; private, unindexed, renamed, or newly launched products may be missed. Product capabilities may change. Reddit anecdotes are self-selected and not representative. This document must be refreshed before positioning or fundraising claims.

## Validation standard
The idea is validated only after target users repeatedly use the report and it measurably changes or accelerates their decisions. Sign-ups and compliments alone are weak evidence.
