# Validation Plan

## Hypothesis
Developers lose meaningful time selecting GitHub issues that are inactive, contested, unclear, or unlikely to receive maintainer attention. An evidence-backed pre-flight report improves their pursue-or-skip decision.

## Participants
Recruit 10–15 developers:
- 5 seeking a first or early open-source contribution;
- 3–5 intermediate contributors;
- 2–5 community mentors or maintainers.

Avoid recruiting only friends or collaborators. Record experience level and prior contribution count, not sensitive personal data.

## Test procedure
1. Ask the participant to bring one real public GitHub issue they are considering.
2. Record their initial decision and confidence before showing the report.
3. Show the report prototype without explaining each section.
4. Ask the participant to think aloud while reading.
5. Record the decision after the report.
6. Ask which evidence changed or failed to change the decision.
7. Ask whether they would analyze a second issue now.
8. Do not promise features or defend the design during the session.

## Metrics
- Decision-change rate.
- Time to reach a decision.
- Report completion/understanding rate.
- Second-analysis intent and observed second analysis.
- Most-used report sections.
- Missing evidence frequency.
- Trust concerns and misunderstood claims.
- Willingness to return; willingness to pay is exploratory only.

## Success criteria
Proceed to MVP backlog when:
- at least 8 of 12 representative participants say the report is clearer than their current process;
- at least 5 decisions materially change or become more confident because of cited evidence;
- at least 6 participants choose to analyze another issue;
- no critical section is misunderstood by more than 20% of participants.

Revise and retest if the problem is real but the report is unclear. Stop or reposition if participants consistently solve the problem quickly with GitHub search alone.

## Ethics and privacy
Use public issues only. Do not collect tokens, private repository content, exact income, or unnecessary identity data. Obtain permission before recording. Store notes under participant codes such as P01.
