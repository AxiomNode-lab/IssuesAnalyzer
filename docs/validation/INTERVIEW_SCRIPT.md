# Interview Script

Target duration: 20–25 minutes.

## Introduction
“We are testing whether an issue pre-flight report helps developers decide where to contribute. We are testing the report, not you. There are no correct answers.”

## Before the report
1. Tell me about the last time you searched for an open-source issue.
2. How did you decide that a project was worth your time?
3. Have you started work and later discovered the issue was inactive, unclear, or already being solved?
4. Looking only at this issue, would you pursue, review further, or skip it?
5. How confident are you from 1–5?

## While reading
1. What do you look at first?
2. What does the score mean to you?
3. Which statements look like facts? Which look like predictions?
4. Is any section confusing or unnecessary?
5. What evidence would you want to verify on GitHub?

## After the report
1. What is your decision now?
2. How confident are you from 1–5?
3. What changed your mind, if anything?
4. What important information is missing?
5. Would you use this for another issue right now?
6. Would you rather paste a link or browse recommendations?
7. What would make you stop trusting the product?
8. Who else has this problem?

## Rules for interviewer
Do not pitch, lead the participant, teach the interface, or ask “Wouldn't this be useful?” Ask for examples from real behavior. Record exact misunderstandings and decision changes.
